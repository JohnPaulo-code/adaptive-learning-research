## Abstract

Over the past few years, higher education institutions have integrated 
the use of computers and online learning, both on and off campus. Adaptive 
learning is a type of learning that involves the use of computer algorithms 
and computers to facilitate and deliver customised learning materials based 
on student preferences. Adaptive Learning can play a major role in improving 
student academic performance.

This study examines the factors that influence the adoption of Adaptive 
Learning Systems in higher education in South Africa, using a case study 
approach comparing data from two private higher education institutes: 
Pearson Institute of Higher Education and IIE Monash South Africa.

**Keywords:** Adaptive learning systems, machine learning, LMS, 
learning styles, higher education, South Africa

## Research Questions

- Which adaptive technologies can be used in higher education?
- What are the requirements for using adaptive technology in higher education?
- What are the advantages of using adaptive learning systems?
- Which people factors influence the use of Adaptive Learning Systems?

## Key Findings

- 71% of students experienced mixed (online + face-to-face) teaching styles
- 65% of students struggled with time management; 57% with assignment burden
- Dominant learning styles: Active (28%), Reflective (22%), Verbal (21%)
- Students strongly perceive adaptive LMS as useful (p = 0.0136)
